1. Welcome

Your job is to reach out to Bangkok clinics that already serve foreign patients and convince them to sign up for our directory partner program. You will work entirely in English. You will only contact clinics that respond to English; we filter the prospect list for you.

Weekly goal: 2 signed partners. If you sign 2 partners per week, in six months we have 50 partners. At an average of 8,000 baht monthly each, that is 400,000 baht in monthly recurring revenue. That is your real measure.

What we sell

We help Bangkok clinics get more international patients through three services:

  1. We list them in our directory at bangkokbotoxclinic.com (aesthetics) or bangkokbestclinic.com (dental). Foreign patients searching Google find us.
  2. When a patient fills out an enquiry form on our site, the lead goes directly to the clinic's LINE messenger.
  3. We give the clinic a private dashboard with AI-drafted replies for their negative Google reviews, plus competitor analysis.

Pricing: Free 30-day trial, no credit card. After the trial, the clinic chooses either 200 baht per closed lead OR 8,000 baht per month for unlimited leads.

The clinic risks nothing. We only get paid when they get paid. This is your core pitch.

2. How outreach works — your role vs. the Thai sales team

You are the front line for the first touch. You send English cold messages to all qualified clinics. Most Bangkok clinics have at least one English-capable person on staff (marketing, manager, owner) who handles foreign enquiries. They will reply if interested.

Two-stage funnel:
Stage 1 — You (English staff): Send initial cold messages in English to every prospect. You handle the conversation if they reply in English.
Stage 2 — Thai sales team: If a clinic replies in Thai or asks for a phone call, you hand off. The Thai team takes over closing.

This means you should send to all qualified clinics, not only those marked English-friendly. The data column has_english_reviewers is a useful signal (Y = you can probably close end-to-end) but not a filter. Send to both Y and N clinics.

When to hand off to the Thai team

  • The clinic replies in Thai language
  • The clinic asks for a phone call (Thai team handles all calls)
  • The reply uses broken English suggesting they need a Thai conversation
  • The clinic asks to meet in person

How to hand off

  • In your tracking sheet, set outcome to HANDOFF_TO_THAI_TEAM
  • Forward the conversation thread to the team lead via LINE or email
  • Include clinic name, dashboard URL, summary of what they asked
  • Stop responding yourself. The Thai team takes over.

You still get the bonus when they sign — you initiated the relationship.

6. Step 2: Picking who to message first

You message all qualified clinics regardless of has_english_reviewers value. Prioritize English-friendly clinics (you handle end-to-end), but send to Thai-only clinics too — hand off to Thai team if they reply in Thai.

Tier One — message TODAY (highest urgency)

Filter: unanswered_neg_reviews ≥ 3 AND recent_reviews_30d ≥ 5

Real problem + active = strong urgency. Use Template Three.

Tier Two — message TODAY (you can close end-to-end)

Filter: has_english_reviewers = Y AND trust_score ≥ 80 AND recent_reviews_30d ≥ 10

High English likelihood. You handle whole conversation. Use Template One.

Tier Three — message tomorrow (may need Thai handoff)

Filter: trust_score ≥ 75 AND recent_reviews_30d ≥ 5, regardless of English flag.

Strong clinics that might reply in Thai. Send English Template One. If reply is Thai → hand off to Thai team.

Tier Four — message later in the week

Mid-tier, district rank 4–10. Long tail.

SKIP these

  • Rating below 4.0 (they have bigger problems than marketing)
  • Total reviews below 30 (too small)
  • Already a partner (check tracking sheet)
  • Contacted in last 7 days (check tracking sheet)

7. Step 3: How to send the message

The hardest part is finding the clinic's LINE ID. Try in this order:

Method 1: Their website

Open the URL in website column. Look for:

  • A LINE QR code (footer or contact page) — scan with phone
  • A LINE ID written as @something — search in LINE
  • A "Book on LINE" button

Method 2: Search LINE app directly

Open LINE → Add Friend → Search → type clinic name. Look for the green-badge verified account.

Method 3: Facebook

Google "[clinic name] facebook" — many post LINE QR in their pinned post.

Method 4: Facebook Messenger

If LINE not findable, message them on Facebook. Mark FB_ONLY in tracking.

Method 5: Website contact form

Last resort. Lowest reply rate. Mark WEBSITE_FORM in tracking.

Once you find their channel: copy the template, fill in variables from CSV, send, and immediately log in tracking sheet. Do not skip the log step.

9. Decision Tree — Which template to use

  1. Has the clinic 3+ unanswered negative reviews?
    • Yes → Template Three
    • No → continue
  2. First time contacting them?
    • Yes → Template One (use Template Two if you already used One that day)
    • Sent T1 three days ago, no reply → Template Four
    • Sent T4 seven days ago, no reply → STOP. Mark dead. Do not contact for 60 days.
  3. They replied with interest?
    • Yes → Template Five. Then add to admin and send dashboard URL. See section 11.

10. Response Handling — Common Objections

"We already do Facebook ads."

Reply: Facebook ads in Bangkok aesthetic average 2,800 baht per acquired lead. Our cost is 200 baht per closed lead. Lower risk because you only pay when the lead converts. You can run both in parallel — complementary, not competing.

"We do not have time to manage another dashboard."

Reply: You do not need to. Alerts come to LINE only when something needs attention. Most active partners log in five minutes per week. Everything else is automated.

"Why should we pay you?"

Reply: Nothing upfront. You only pay when a lead closes. We carry the risk. If we cannot deliver leads you can close, you owe nothing.

"How much exactly?"

Reply: 30-day trial free. After: 200 baht per closed lead OR 8,000 baht/month unlimited. Most start pay-per-lead and switch to monthly when they close 40+ per month.

"Do we need to sign a contract?"

Reply: No contract. No commitment. Cancel anytime via LINE.

"Let me think about it."

Reply: Of course. The trial is free and you can cancel anytime, so there is nothing to risk. The most informative thing is to start the trial, see what comes in for 30 days, and decide then.

"We are not interested."

Reply: Understood. Thanks for taking the time. If anything changes, I am here. Wishing you a strong year.

Mark "dead — not interested". Move on.

"Can we talk on the phone?"

Reply: Of course. When works best? Fifteen minutes is plenty.

If they go silent after expressing interest

Wait 2 days. Send: "Hi, just checking in. Still interested? If you are ready, send me your LINE ID and email and we will activate your trial immediately."

No reply in 3 more days → mark "ghosted". Move on.

11. Closing — Activating a New Partner

When a clinic agrees to start a trial, get three pieces of information:

  1. LINE User ID (begins with U)
  2. Email address (for weekly summaries)
  3. Approximate average procedure ticket value in baht (for ROI math)

Then in the admin panel:

  1. Log in at /admin
  2. Click Partners tab
  3. Click + Add Partner
  4. Search for the clinic by name — autocomplete will show matches. Click correct one.
  5. Fill form: Plan tier Trial, Monthly fee 0, Avg ticket their value, Contact email, LINE user ID
  6. Click Add partner
  7. Find the partner in the list. Click 📋 Copy dashboard link.

Send the URL back to them:

Your dashboard is ready and your 30-day trial is live. {DASHBOARD_URL} Bookmark this page. Any patient who fills out the enquiry form on our directory will appear in your dashboard and trigger a LINE notification to you. I will check in at the 14-day mark to see how things are going. Anything you need before then, just LINE me here.

After 30 days: Check Partners tab → see lead count. If they got leads and closed some, ask to convert to paid. If zero leads, talk honestly — extend trial or part amicably.

14. Frequently Asked Questions

I cannot find the clinic's LINE ID anywhere.

Try in this order: search LINE directly with the clinic name, check Google Maps photos for a QR code, check Facebook page (especially pinned posts), or use Facebook Messenger as backup.

My LINE account got temporarily restricted.

You sent too many too quickly. Future: max 25/day, wait 5 seconds between messages, vary wording, spread across time of day. Restricted now? Wait 24 hours before resuming.

A clinic was rude or hostile.

Do not engage. Reply: "Thank you for the response. Wishing your clinic success." Mark dead — hostile. Never contact again.

They asked a technical question I cannot answer.

Be honest. Reply: "Let me check with our technical team and get back to you within 24 hours." Ask team lead. Never make up answers.

They want to negotiate the price.

Pricing is firm. Only flexibility: extend trial from 30 to 60 days for clinics that ask. That is it.

They ask about invoices/tax documentation.

Reply: "We auto-generate tax invoices for every payment, sent to the email on file." Confirm with team lead before clinic's first payment.

They want more proof of how the system works.

Send demo dashboard: bangkokbotoxclinic.com/dashboard/demo or bangkokbestclinic.com/dashboard/demo. If they want more, ask team lead for a recorded walkthrough.

I see a clinic that we tried 2 months ago and they said no.

Check tracking. If dead: skip. After 60+ days you may retry with a fresh angle.

The dashboard URL does not load for them.

LINE truncates long URLs. Try sending it on its own line, without https:// prefix, or send a screenshot plus the URL separately.

How do we compare to other Bangkok directories?

Be direct: Other directories operate as pay-to-rank. We rank by Trust Score derived from actual Google review data. Our partners pay only for delivered leads, not for placement. If we do not deliver leads, the partner pays nothing.
